Boundary: this browser tool reviews information you supply. It does not connect to physical instruments, discover sensors, certify scientific truth, measure joules, or authorize action. A completed review means only “complete within declared scope.”

01 · Claim and review boundary

State what is being evaluated before reviewing it.

02 · Three-ring review

Each ring can support, challenge, or remain unresolved.

RING 01 / ASSUMPTIONS

Philosophy

Are terms, assumptions, values and scope explicit? Is the claim internally coherent?

RING 02 / OBSERVATION

Physics

Can the claim be tied to methods, units, uncertainty, limits and traceable observations?

RING 03 / CONSTRAINT

Law

Are formal rules, operational permissions and applicable human-made laws distinguished?

03 · Spectrum and realm coverage

Coverage is explicit, bounded, and never assumed complete.

These six review axes are a classification scaffold, not a claim that all phenomena fit neatly into six buckets. Select a state and enter the method, reason or evidence reference. “Measured” without evidence is blocked.
